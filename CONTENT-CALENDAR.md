# Content calendar

Topic ideas for this site. When the website agent is switched on, it adds
new ideas each Monday under "Waiting for your answer". It writes a piece
only after you say yes.

## How to answer
1. Press the pencil at the top right of this file on GitHub.
2. After "Your answer:" type `yes` or `no`. You can add a note after it,
   for example `yes, keep it short`.
3. Press "Commit changes".

Or tell Claude, for example "yes to the first topic on Street Art
Dispatch", and it will fill this in for you.
