From the editors

Art Doesn’t Always Wait for an Invitation.

Street Art Dispatch follows artists taking their work beyond conventional gallery spaces. From public interventions and urban installations to experimental materials and collectable artworks, we document the evolving relationship between contemporary art, the city and its audience.
